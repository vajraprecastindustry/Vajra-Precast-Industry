Who We Are

15 Years of Uncompromising Quality and Strength.

Vajra Precast Industry stands as a symbol of strength and durability, just like a thunderbolt. We are a registered, legally compliant company, pioneering the manufacture of high-grade compressed concrete interlocking tiles, blocks, and precast infrastructure solutions.

Operating primarily in Patna, Bihar, our manufacturing processes utilize modern hydraulic compression techniques ensuring every block is built to withstand extreme weather, heavy loads, and the test of time. Whether it is a beautifully landscaped park, an industrial driveway, or a secure residential boundary, Vajra delivers excellence.

Our Products

Engineered for aesthetics, engineered for strength.

Interlocking Paver Blocks

Heavy-duty, precision-crafted compressed interlocking tiles. Ideal for driveways, footpaths, petrol pumps, and parks. Available in various colors and locking patterns to resist shifting under vehicular load.

Curb & Edging Stones

Crucial for road infrastructure and landscaping. Our curb stones neatly separate pavements from roads and garden beds, preventing the lateral movement of installed paver blocks.

Solid & Hollow Concrete Blocks

The modern alternative to red bricks. Provides excellent structural strength, high thermal insulation, and rapid construction speeds for both load-bearing and non-load-bearing walls.

Fly Ash Bricks

Eco-friendly, highly durable, and cost-effective. These uniformly shaped bricks offer high compressive strength, require less mortar, and significantly reduce plastering costs.

RCC Precast Boundary Walls

A fast, robust, and aesthetic security solution. Prefabricated Reinforced Cement Concrete panels that lock into pillars. Weather-proof, secure, and visually appealing compared to standard brick walls.

Fencing Poles & Pillars

Heavy-duty RCC fencing poles designed for agricultural boundaries, commercial plots, and secure facilities. Impervious to rust and termites, ensuring lifelong security.
